Due diligence, for the person joining

Before you join a startup, see what's true about it.

When a VC invests in a startup, they spend weeks checking it out. When you join one, you bet years of your career on it and take part of your pay in equity, usually after one call with the founder and a look at the website.

Clarity checks what a company says about itself against its code, hiring, news and pricing, then writes a short memo: what holds up, what's moving, what nobody has said publicly, and what to ask. Claims and signals are footnoted to their sources.
