Clarity for candidates

Before you join a startup, see what's true about it.

When a VC invests in a startup, they spend weeks checking it out. When you join one, you bet years of your career on it and take part of your pay in equity, usually after a single call with the founder and a look at the website.

Clarity checks what a company says about itself against its code, hiring, news and pricing. You get a short memo: what holds up, what's moving, what nobody has said publicly, and what to ask. Each claim and signal links to its source.
